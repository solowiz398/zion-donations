How we use your gift

We believe donors deserve to know where their money goes. Here is how donations support the children in our home.

Shelter

Housing and upkeep

Rent or repairs for the home, beds and bedding, clean water, electricity and cleaning supplies so the children live somewhere safe and comfortable.

Education

School and learning

School fees, uniforms, shoes, exercise books, stationery and after-school tutoring for children of every age.

Nutrition

Food and water

Groceries for three meals a day, snacks, and clean drinking water, with extra support for children who need special diets.

Healthcare

Health and wellbeing

Routine checkups, vaccinations, medicine, hygiene supplies and transport to clinics. We also support counselling so children can heal emotionally.

Care

Caregivers and daily life

Caregiver and teacher support, clothing, play and recreation, and activities that help each child build confidence and friendships.

Running

Keeping the home operating

Basic administration, insurance and the record-keeping needed to care for children responsibly and stay accountable to our donors.

Our commitment to transparency

Every donation is recorded, and donors can ask us questions at any time.

Donations received through cashapp, paypal or bitcoin. We keep records of how funds are spent, and we are happy to share a summary with any donor who asks.

Given the current situation, Bitcoin is a more convenient payment option because it allows donations to be transferred easily without relying on traditional banking channels.
