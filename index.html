About us

Zion Orphanage was founded in 1899 by Rabbi Avraham Yochanan Blumenthal in Jerusalem. It was established during a period when poverty, disease, hunger and difficult living conditions left many Jewish children without adequate parental care.

Over more than a century, Zion Orphanage has helped thousands of children build better lives. Some of its former residents have gone on to become business leaders, professionals and members of the Israeli parliament (Knesset).

Its central philosophy remains that every child deserves a safe home, proper education and an opportunity to reach their full potential.

Donations are used directly for the children's needs, and we are always happy to share how funds are spent.
